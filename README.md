# 🚀 ORBIT — Connecting Education, People & Opportunities

**ORBIT** is a modern educational platform designed to connect **students, teachers, and schools** in one seamless digital ecosystem.

Our mission is to make education more accessible, engaging, and connected while creating meaningful opportunities for educators and educational institutions.

## 🌟 About the Project

ORBIT goes beyond traditional online learning platforms. It brings together three essential parts of the educational community:

### 🎓 For Students
- Discover educational content and learning resources.
- Enjoy an interactive and personalized learning experience.
- Track learning progress and stay motivated.
- Learn in an organized, engaging environment.

### 👨‍🏫 For Teachers
- Build a professional teaching profile.
- Showcase skills, experience, and expertise.
- Share educational content and connect with students.
- Increase professional visibility and explore career opportunities.

### 🏫 For Schools
- Discover talented and qualified educators.
- Explore teacher profiles and professional experience.
- Simplify the process of finding suitable teaching candidates.
- Connect with educators who match their institutional needs.

## ✨ Platform Vision

ORBIT aims to create a connected educational ecosystem where learning, teaching, and professional growth come together.

The platform is designed with a modern, responsive interface and a focus on accessibility, usability, and user experience.

## 🎨 Design Goals

- Modern, intuitive, and responsive user interface.
- Arabic and English language support (RTL/LTR).
- Dark and Light modes.
- Personalized experiences for different user roles.
- Engaging design that makes learning enjoyable without unnecessary complexity.

## 🎯 Our Mission

**Empowering students to learn, teachers to grow, and schools to discover talent — all in one ORBIT.**
