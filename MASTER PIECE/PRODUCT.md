# The Learning Orbit
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
Web, existing vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript application using Vite and Three.js.
## Users
Students learn and join channels; teachers publish and manage educational channels; schools manage recruitment and their workflows. Administrator routes retain existing access checks.
## Product Purpose
An educational platform connecting learning materials, practical experiments, teachers and professional opportunities.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing routes, authentication, roles, publishing, membership, seats, physics and stored-data contracts. Preserve the homepage Three.js hero composition and renderer, including its shared dependencies; localization may update displayed copy. Current client-side private-channel checks are not a server authorization guarantee. Do not fabricate activity or expose private classroom information on public profiles. Profile edits use the existing identity and persistence mechanism. Settings expose only implemented controls. Students discover teachers through the existing directory/channel experience.
## Confirmed Brand Commitments
Replace the former violet-heavy platform identity with midnight blue and learning blue on neutral foundations. Students use turquoise, yellow and coral; teachers use teal with a darker accessible action shade; schools use deep indigo. Preserve the protected homepage hero's existing artwork and local art palette. Scientific space exploration, distinct purposeful finite motion, readable Arabic and English, one visible language control, responsive layouts and reduced-motion/calm preferences remain commitments. No framework migration or duplicate animation dependencies. Registration uses one locally bundled decorative Three scene with graceful fallback. The existing fictional login illustration retains its provenance; no new raster assets are required.
## Evidence
The attached explicit redesign request supersedes the earlier violet requirement. The approved code-led direction is recorded in the external Codex workspace at `outputs/rebrand/direction-contract.md`; canonical visual tokens and usage are in `DESIGN.md` with extensions in `.impeccable/design.json`. Existing Phase 2/3 reports and `outputs/rebrand/signup-checks.json`, `signup-flow-checks.json` and `home-auth-checks.json` describe tested limits. Registration lifecycle, calm/reduced motion, fallback and supported role destinations were exercised in an isolated browser; homepage WebGL/moon rendering was unavailable in that context, while fallback, localization and source preservation were checked. Synthetic lifecycle checks and browser coverage do not establish real-device GPU performance, backend authorization or production security.
